# LumaLilt Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

LumaLilt is developed by Jonnathan R. MacKinnon, MacKinnonTech.

LumaLilt does not collect or transmit personal information. It has no account registration,
advertisements, analytics SDKs, tracking, or network requests. Puzzle generation happens on your device.

The app stores puzzle progress, completed-puzzle history, and appearance preferences locally.
Your device's backup service may include this information according to your Apple backup settings.
LumaLilt does not operate a separate cloud synchronization service.

If you choose to share a completed result, the app provides the result text to the destination
you select in Apple's share sheet. That destination handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy.

You can clear puzzle history and saved puzzles using Settings → Reset all puzzle progress.
Appearance settings remain until changed or until the app's local data is removed.

This policy must be updated before adding any feature that changes these practices.
