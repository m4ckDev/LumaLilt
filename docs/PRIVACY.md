# LumaLilt Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

LumaLilt is developed by Jonnathan R. MacKinnon, MacKinnonTech.

## Information handled by the app

LumaLilt does not collect or transmit personal information to MacKinnonTech. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, or tracking. Puzzle generation and gameplay take place on your device without a server connection.

The app stores puzzle progress, completed-puzzle history, and appearance preferences locally. MacKinnonTech cannot access those records. Your device's backup service may include this information according to your Apple backup settings. LumaLilt does not operate a separate cloud synchronization service.

## Sharing and external pages

If you choose to share a completed result, LumaLilt provides the result text to the destination you select in Apple's share sheet. The selected service handles that text under its own privacy policy.

Opening the Privacy Policy link opens a page hosted by GitHub in your browser. GitHub's handling of website visits is described in [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Your puzzle records are not included in that link.

## Retention and deletion

Local puzzle records remain until you reset progress or remove the app's local data. Use Settings → Reset all puzzle progress to clear saved puzzles, completion history, and milestones. Appearance settings remain until changed or the app's local data is removed. Copies in device backups follow your Apple backup settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, email [jonnathan.mackinnon@mackinnontech.com](mailto:jonnathan.mackinnon@mackinnontech.com).

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable recordkeeping obligations, and do not use it for advertising. You can request deletion by emailing the same address.

## Policy changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if LumaLilt's privacy practices change.
